Who We Are

Jovika Foods Fresh Produce Co (also known as Jovika Foods Farm-to-Table Limited) is a pioneering FoodTech company based in the heart of Ogori/Magongo, Nigeria. Founded by a passionate confectionery tech entrepreneur, we bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern food technology.

Our mission is simple: to deliver the freshest produce and finest confectionery products directly from our farms to your table. We work closely with local farmers, ensuring sustainable practices while maintaining the highest quality standards. Every product that leaves our facility is a testament to our commitment to freshness, quality, and customer satisfaction.

At Jovika Foods, we believe that good food starts with good farming. That's why we've invested in both the agricultural heritage of Ogori/Magongo and cutting-edge food technology to bring you the best of both worlds.

What We Offer

Fresh Farm Produce

Directly from our partner farms in Ogori/Magongo, we supply the freshest vegetables, fruits, and herbs. Our farm-to-table model ensures you receive produce at peak freshness, harvested and delivered within hours.

Premium Confectionery

Leveraging our expertise in confectionery technology, we create delicious baked goods, pastries, and sweet treats using locally-sourced ingredients. From traditional Nigerian favorites to contemporary delights, every bite is crafted with care.

Farm-to-Table Delivery

Our innovative delivery system brings farm freshness directly to your home or business. We use temperature-controlled logistics to maintain quality from harvest to your kitchen.

Bulk Supply for Businesses

Restaurants, hotels, and food businesses can rely on us for consistent, high-quality produce and confectionery supplies. We offer flexible ordering and reliable delivery schedules tailored to your business needs.
