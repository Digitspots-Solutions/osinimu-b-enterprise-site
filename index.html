About Us

OSINIMU B ENTERPRISE is a leading fashion and creative design house based in the heart of Ogori/Magongo, Nigeria. We specialize in creating stunning, made-to-measure garments and innovative design solutions that celebrate Nigerian culture while embracing modern trends. Our commitment to quality craftsmanship, attention to detail, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in fashion across the community. Whether you're looking for traditional attire, contemporary fashion pieces, or creative design services, our experienced team transforms your ideas into wearable art.

Our Services

Custom Fashion Design

We create bespoke clothing tailored perfectly to your measurements and style preferences, from elegant aso-ebi to contemporary casual wear.

Traditional Nigerian Attire

Expert crafting of traditional outfits including agbada, buba and sokoto, iro and buba, and ceremonial wear for weddings and special occasions.

Contemporary Fashion

Modern clothing designs that blend international trends with Nigerian flair, perfect for the fashion-forward individual.

Alterations & Repairs

Professional garment alterations, resizing, and repairs to keep your favorite pieces fitting perfectly.

Creative Design Consultation

Personalized style consultation to help you discover looks that enhance your natural beauty and confidence.

Event Fashion

Complete outfit coordination and creation for weddings, parties, corporate events, and special celebrations.
